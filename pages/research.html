The thread through my work is whether a biological conclusion actually follows from the data that produced it. What I want to find out next is the repair-or-die decision: after a Cas9 break, whether a cell repairs or dies, whether the structure of the break and the manner of its delivery shape that commitment, and whether the decision is made cell by cell or coordinated across neighbours (more).

Research Projects

TRIPinRNA: RNA Triple Helix Prediction

Co-developed (co-first author) an in silico platform that searches the human transcriptome for intramolecular RNA triple helices, and performed the bioinformatics analysis; its predictions include triplex-forming sequences in lncRNAs involved in X-chromosome inactivation.

View Project Details

StickForStats Platform Development

An open-source React/Django web platform whose Guardian engine runs the relevant subset of its eight assumption validators before a test executes and, on critical violations, reroutes to an appropriate nonparametric alternative. MIT-licensed and validated against SciPy and R. It runs at stickforstats.com.

bioRxiv preprint (DOI: 10.64898/2026.06.15.732278), in peer review at BMC Bioinformatics. First and corresponding author.

View Project Details

Checkability Audit

A pre-registered audit of whether published biomedical statistics can be recomputed at all. With the protocol frozen before any data were fetched, 3,000 randomly sampled PubMed Central Open Access research articles (2020–2025) were checked for results whose p-value can be recomputed from the printed test statistic and degrees of freedom, and every frozen estimate was re-estimated out of sample on a further 20,000 articles. The measurement pipeline is deterministic, with no machine learning in it, so every number reproduces from committed code.

Manuscript in preparation. First and corresponding author.

Retrieval-augmented research assistant for internal lab documents and the primary literature: keyword (BM25) retrieval over a Weaviate index, with an LLM answering from the top passages; the source documents are listed under each answer.
