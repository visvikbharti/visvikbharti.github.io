Research & Publications

The thread through my work is whether a biological conclusion actually follows from the data that produced it. What I want to find out next is the repair-or-die decision: after a Cas9 break, whether a cell repairs or dies, whether the structure of the break and the manner of its delivery shape that commitment, and whether the decision is made cell by cell or coordinated across neighbours (more).

Research Overview

I am a bioinformatician specializing in computational genomics, RNA biology, and statistical tool development. As a Project Associate-II at the Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (CSIR-IGIB) under Dr. Debojyoti Chakraborty, I build computational and statistical methods for genome engineering and RNA biology, working on RNA structure analysis, DNA repair mechanisms, and tool development for researchers.

Research Projects

TRIPinRNA: RNA Triple Helix Prediction

Co-developed (co-first author) an in silico platform for predicting intramolecular RNA triple helix structures, with applications in understanding X chromosome inactivation and gene regulation mechanisms.

View Project Details

StickForStats Platform Development

An open-source React/Django web platform whose Guardian engine runs eight assumption validators before a test executes and, on critical violations, reroutes to an appropriate nonparametric alternative. MIT-licensed and validated against SciPy and R.

bioRxiv preprint (DOI: 10.64898/2026.06.15.732278), in peer review at BMC Bioinformatics. First and corresponding author.

View Project Details

DNA Repair Mechanisms Analysis

Developed a three-layer computational pipeline to decipher repair factor requirements in staggered versus blunt-end DNA breaks using scRNA-seq data and advanced statistical methods.

View Project Details

Human Forebrain Assembloids

Conducting computational analyses of RNA-seq data from human forebrain assembloids to understand interneuron migration disorders and their implications for neurodevelopmental conditions.

RNA Lab Navigator

Retrieval-augmented research assistant over internal lab documents and the primary literature, for fast, grounded literature triage in a wet-dry lab. In routine use by about 21 researchers.

View Project Details

G-Quadruplexes in Gene Regulation

Computational and proteomics analysis of how IGF2BP1 regulates CCN1 expression by binding a G-quadruplex structure in its 3′ UTR (published in Biochemistry, ACS).

Research Journey

2023-Present

Project Associate-II at CSIR-IGIB

Co-developed TRIPinRNA (co-first author), designed and built the computational arm of a single-cell DNA repair study, and built the StickForStats platform. Working under the supervision of Dr. Debojyoti Chakraborty.

2022

Junior Research Fellow at IIT Guwahati

Carried out a hybrid proteomics-cheminformatics-network-medicine analysis of Ashwagandha and Yograj Guggulu for osteoarthritis (protein-protein-interaction analysis and computational drug repurposing). Worked under the supervision of Dr. Vibin Ramakrishnan.

2021-2022

Master's Thesis at IIT Guwahati

Conducted RNA-seq analysis to classify transcripts susceptible to Nonsense Mediated mRNA Decay (NMD) and explored cellular behaviors in the absence of the UPF3B gene. Worked under the supervision of Dr. Kusum K. Singh.

2017-2018

Bachelor's Thesis at IEM Kolkata

Created a platform integrating NCBI BLAST and PDB databases for protein image retrieval and detailed analysis using Jupyter Notebook and API implementation. Worked under the supervision of Prof. Indranil Basu.
